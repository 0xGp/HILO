04 / 08

How it works

Vaulted entry. Committed shoe. Player extract.

Economics

20% / 80% at deposit

0.2 HILO to treasury. 0.8 HILO vaulted as player credit. Extract rate: 3 HP = 0.1 HILO.

Fairness

HMAC-SHA256 shoe

Seed committed before play. Deck shuffled with HMAC draws. Verified around each call—not Math.random.

Clock

20-second match

One clock for the round. Time-up with HP: bank or rebuy. Wipe at 0: rebuy only.

Edges

Ace & Two locked

Ace has no higher. Two has no lower. Same rank = miss. Rules stay on the table.
