04

HOW HILO WORKS

01
Connect wallet. Buy-in is 1 HILO ($20). 20% (0.2 HILO) is sent to 0x974aB06E37dfD2d27FAc09c0E6194d2E13042DcC.
02
A card is dealt. Choose HIGHER or LOWER.
03
Hit: bank +$23. $20 becomes $43.
04
Miss or same rank: bank −$3 off that $20. $20 becomes $17. You are not wiped unless the bank hits $0.
05
Keep calling while anything is left. Withdraw sends remaining HILO to your wallet.
06
$0 bank = round over. Deposit another $20 HILO to play again.
